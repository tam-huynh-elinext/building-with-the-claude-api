# Nutrition Plan for Dual Training & Competition Day

## **Daily Calorie Target**
- **BMR (~1,700 cal) + Activity (×1.7) = ~2,900 cal**
- Adjust to **3,100-3,300 cal** for optimal recovery between sessions

---

## **MORNING (Pre-9 AM Training)**
*2-3 hours before practice*
- **Oatmeal** (50g) + banana + honey + pinch of salt
- **Greek yogurt** (150g)
- Water + electrolytes

**Macros:** 50g carbs, 15g protein, 8g fat

---

## **Mid-Morning (Post-Training Recovery)**
*Within 30 minutes of 10:30 AM finish*
- **Protein shake:** Whey protein (25g) + banana + 250ml whole milk
- **Toast with almond butter** (1-2 slices)

**Macros:** 50g carbs, 35g protein, 10g fat

---

## **LUNCH (12-1 PM)**
- **Lean protein:** 150-180g chicken/fish/lean beef
- **Rice or pasta:** 150-200g (cooked)
- **Vegetables:** Mixed greens, broccoli, sweet potato
- Olive oil drizzle

**Macros:** 70g carbs, 45g protein, 12g fat

---

## **Afternoon Snack (4-5 PM)**
*2 hours before evening match*
- **Rice cakes** or **bagel** with jam
- **Banana** + handful of almonds
- Water

**Macros:** 60g carbs, 10g protein, 8g fat

---

## **EVENING (Post-Competition)**
*Within 30 minutes of match end*
- **Protein + carbs:** Chicken sandwich, chocolate milk, or pasta with lean meat
- **Hydrate:** 500-750ml water + electrolytes

**Macros:** 50g carbs, 35g protein, 10g fat

---

## **DINNER (8:30-9 PM)**
- **Salmon or lean beef:** 150g
- **Sweet potato or brown rice:** 150g

- **Healthy fat:** Avocado or olive oil

**Macros:** 50g carbs, 40g protein, 15g fat

---

## **Hydration Strategy**
- **Morning:** 500ml upon waking
- **Pre-training:** 400-500ml
- **During both sessions:** 150-200ml every 15 min
- **Post-activity:** 150% of sweat loss over 4-6 hours
- **Throughout day:** 3-4 liters total

---

## **Key Principles**
✅ Prioritize **carbs + protein** around both sessions  
✅ Sodium in meals/drinks aids hydration & performance  
✅ Avoid high fiber/fat 2-3 hours before exercise  
✅ Sleep 7-9 hours for optimal recovery
