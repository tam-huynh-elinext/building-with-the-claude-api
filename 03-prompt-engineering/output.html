## 1. DAILY CALORIE NEEDS

| **Fat** | 80-95g | 720-855 | 25-28% |

*Higher carbs support endurance; adequate protein aids recovery*

---

## 3. MEAL TIMING AROUND WORKOUTS

### **Morning (6-7 AM) - Before Long Runs**
- 2-3 hours prior: Oatmeal + banana + almond butter
- 30 min prior: Banana or energy gel

### **During Run (>90 min)**
- 6-8g carbs/minute
- Sports drink, gels, or chews

### **Post-Workout (within 30-60 min) - CRITICAL**
- Chocolate milk, smoothie, or rice + chicken
- 1.2g carbs/kg body weight (82g)
- 0.25g protein/kg body weight (17g)

### **Other Meals**
- Breakfast, lunch, dinner spread throughout day
- Snacks as needed
